# Tone of voice — engelsk

Udtrukket af wiebendesign.dk's engelske sider (september 2026): Exhibition
stands, Solutions, Graphics and print, LED screens, Storage, Process og FAQ.
Bruges som rettesnor for den engelske version af beregneren.

## Ordet for produktet

**"Exhibition stand" — aldrig "booth".** Sitet bruger "exhibition stand"
gennemgående. Begivenheden hedder både **"exhibition"** og **"trade show"**
("Have a trade show coming up?", "trade show organizer"), men produktet er
altid en *stand*.

> "We design and build exhibition stands around your brand, products, and goals
> – and manage the entire process."

## Stavning: amerikansk

Sitet skriver `color`, `meter`, `recognizable`, `organizer`, `organized`,
`authorized`. Der er ét enkelt britisk udslip ("centre stage"), som ikke skal
kopieres. **Beregneren bruger amerikansk stavning konsekvent:** color, meter,
organize, authorize, recognize.

## Person

**"You" og "your" om kunden, "we" om Wieben.** Dansk bruger flertals-"I/jeres";
engelsk bruger ental-"you". Samme nøgterne register, ingen udråbstegn, ingen
superlativer.

## Treklangen

Dansk: *"jeres brand, produkter og mål"*.
Engelsk: **"your brand, products, and goals"** — går igen mindst fire steder
på sitet. Bemærk Oxford-kommaet.

## Den bløde afslutning

Dansk: *"Så ser vi på mulighederne og tager dialogen derfra."*
Engelsk, sitets egen: **"Tell us what you have in mind and what you need your
stand to do. We'll explore the possibilities and take it from there."**

Knappen hedder **"Tell us about your project"**.

## Forløbet — sitets fem faser

Beregnerens tidslinje skal følge dem:

1. **Goals & requirements**
2. **Design concept & project plan**
3. **Production & coordination**
4. **Delivery & installation**
5. **Dismantling & storage**

Nyttige formuleringer derfra:
- *"Once we have access to the exhibitor portal, we take care of the relevant
  orders and deadlines."* → tidslinjens "Bestillinger hos messearrangøren".
  Ordet er **exhibitor portal**.
- *"Before the doors open, we inspect the stand and take care of the final
  details. By the time your team arrives, everything is in place, leaving you
  free to focus on the exhibition."* → "Opbygning og messedage".
- *"We recommend starting around six months before the exhibition, or earlier.
  As the date approaches, supplier deadlines can result in higher costs and
  fewer options."* → det faglige tip "Der er kort tid til". Bemærk, at sitet
  selv siger **six months**, samme som den danske.

## Grafikniveauerne

Sitet har den direkte pendant til den danske sætning, beregnerens tre niveauer
er bygget på:

> "Get help with the full graphic design process or only the part needed to make
> your artwork production-ready."

Det giver niveauerne:

| Nøgle | Engelsk titel |
|---|---|
| `klar` | We have production-ready files |
| `delvis` | We have a logo and images |
| `alt` | We need help with all of it |

Og sitets egne understøttende formuleringer: *"Our graphic designers develop and
adapt the artwork to fit the format, surface and production method"*, *"We
adjust the format, proportions and layout so your graphics work where they will
be used"*.

## Om firmaet — sitets egne engelske formuleringer

Fra Om os-siden:

| Dansk | Engelsk, ordret fra sitet |
|---|---|
| Vi bygger med overblik og omtanke | **We build with clarity and care** |
| et team, der samler projektets mange dele | The team bringing the whole project together |
| Mere end 30 års erfaring | **Over 30 years of trade show experience** / *For more than 30 years* |
| messer i mere end 70 lande | We have supported clients at exhibitions in **more than 70 countries** |
| lagerfaciliteter i både Danmark og USA | **warehouse facilities in both Denmark and the United States** |

Bemærk **"the United States"**, ikke "USA". Kunden hedder **"client"**.

Beregnerens forsidelinje bliver derfor:
**"Over 30 years of trade show experience · exhibitions in more than 70 countries"**

### Værdierne

Dansk: overblik, fleksibilitet, kreativitet, ansvar for detaljerne.
Engelsk, sitets egne: **CLARITY, FLEXIBILITY, CREATIVITY, RESPONSIBILITY**.

Nyttige vendinger derfra:
- *"We create a clear path through each project, so you always know what is
  happening, what comes next, and who is responsible."* — det er nøjagtig
  beregnerens løfte om overblik.
- *"We explore the possibilities and develop a solution that gives your business
  a distinct expression while still working in practice."*
- *"solutions that work in practice and a process that remains clear and
  manageable from start to finish"*

### Endnu en blød afslutning

> showroom, or project. We'll talk through what you need and how we can best
> help."
